About Me

The story, skills, and approach behind Forr3ver Dev.

My Journey

I got into web development the way a lot of people do — by trying to build something and getting hooked on the process. What started as curiosity turned into consistent practice: building landing pages, breaking things, fixing them, and slowly getting better at turning an idea into something people can actually use. Right now, I'm sharpening my skills through a software development program while working hands-on as a developer, and I make it a habit to build something new almost every week — a feature, a layout, a small tool — just to keep learning by doing. I'm not chasing perfection; I'm chasing steady, visible improvement, one project at a time.

How I Approach Work

Clarity over complexity

I'd rather ship something simple that works than something clever that confuses people.

Honesty in scope

I tell you what's realistic, not just what sounds good, so there are no surprises later.

Details matter

Spacing, load speed, mobile experience — the small things are what make a site feel finished.

Built to be used

A website isn't a showpiece, it's a tool. I build with the end user in mind, not just the client's approval.

How I Work

Discover

I start by understanding what you actually need — your goals, your audience, and what the site has to do for your business. No assumptions, no guesswork.

Build

I design and code with intention, keeping you updated as the site takes shape. Clean, mobile-first, and built to actually work — not just look good.

Launch

Once everything's tested and polished, I deploy it live and hand it over — ready to bring in customers from day one.
