How long does a project take?

Timelines depend on the tier — a single landing page typically takes 3-5 days, while a full custom build can take 7-10 days. I'll confirm an exact timeline once I understand your project's scope.

Do I need to provide the content (text, images) myself?

It helps if you can provide your own text and images, but it's not required — I can help write copy and source placeholder visuals to get things moving, and we can swap in your final content before launch.

Can I get the source code?

Yes, source code handover is included with the Premium tier, or available as an add-on for other tiers if you'd like full ownership of the codebase.

What if I need changes after the site is delivered?

Every project includes revisions before final delivery. If you need changes after launch, I'm happy to help — just reach out and we'll sort out the scope together.

Do you offer hosting/domain setup?

I can guide you through hosting and domain setup, or handle deployment for you as part of the project. Let me know what you need and we'll figure out the right approach.

How does payment work?

Projects start with a deposit before work begins, with the remaining balance due on delivery before final handover. I'll walk you through the specifics when we discuss your project.
